Privacy Policy

Last Updated: June 17, 2026

01. Overview

This Privacy Policy explains what information Vicifa ("the Service", "we", "us") collects when you connect your TikTok account, why we collect it, and how it is used. By using the Service you agree to the practices described here.

02. Information We Collect

When you sign in with TikTok Login Kit (OAuth 2.0), TikTok shares the following with us, limited strictly to the scopes we request:

  • Basic profile information (from the user.info.basic scope) — your TikTok display name, username, and profile picture, used only to show you which account is connected.
  • OAuth access and refresh tokens — used solely to call the TikTok API on your explicit request, for example when you choose to upload a video draft.

We do not request or receive your TikTok password. We do not access your TikTok followers, messages, or analytics.

03. How We Use Your Information

We use the information above only to operate the Service for you: to keep you signed in, to show which TikTok account is connected, and to upload a video you choose as a private draft to your own TikTok account (via the video.upload scope) when you ask us to. We never use your information for advertising, and we never sell or rent your data to third parties.

04. What Vicifa Does Not Do

To be explicit: Vicifa does not publish or share anything to TikTok on your behalf, does not use the video.publish scope, does not run any scheduled or automated posting, does not generate content or synthesize voice using AI tools, and does not integrate with TikTok Shop. Publishing a draft to your TikTok feed is always a manual action you take yourself inside the TikTok app.

05. Third-Party Services

We share data only with the following third parties, and only as needed to operate the Service:

  • TikTok API — to authenticate you and to upload video drafts you request. Governed by TikTok's own Privacy Policy.
  • GitHub Pages — used only to host these static, public informational pages (this Privacy Policy and our Terms of Service). GitHub Pages does not receive your TikTok data.

We do not use any workflow-automation platform (such as n8n) or any voice-synthesis or AI content-generation service in connection with your TikTok account or data.

06. Data Storage & Security

Your data is stored using industry-standard security practices and encrypted in transit. We retain your data only for as long as your account remains connected to the Service, or as required by law.

07. Your Rights & Account Control

You may revoke Vicifa's access to your TikTok account at any time from your TikTok app (Settings → Security → Manage account access) or by disconnecting your account inside Vicifa. You may also request that we delete your stored data by contacting us. Revoking access immediately stops any further API calls on your behalf.

08. Children's Privacy

The Service is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect data from children under 13.

09. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Continued use of the Service after changes are posted constitutes acceptance of the revised policy.

10. Contact

Questions about this Privacy Policy or your data can be sent to ifa403@gmail.com.
