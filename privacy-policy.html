Privacy Policy

Section 01

Introduction

Vicifa Content App ("we," "us," or "our") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and safeguard your information when you use our TikTok content automation platform.

This policy applies to all users of the Vicifa Content App and covers data obtained through our TikTok API integration.

Section 02

Information We Collect

We collect the following types of information:

Data Type Source Purpose
TikTok Username & Display Name TikTok API (user.info.basic) Display your identity within the App
TikTok User ID TikTok API Authenticate and link your account
OAuth Access Token TikTok OAuth Publish content on your behalf
Video Content User-provided Upload and publish to TikTok
Publishing Logs App activity Track publishing history

Section 03

How We Use Your Information

We use your information exclusively to:

  • Authenticate your TikTok account via OAuth
  • Publish content to your TikTok account as instructed by you
  • Display your account information within the App interface
  • Maintain logs of publishing activity for your reference
  • Improve App performance and fix technical issues
We Never Sell Your Data

Your personal information and TikTok data are never sold, rented, or shared with third parties for marketing purposes.

Section 04

TikTok API Data Usage

In compliance with TikTok's API Terms of Service, we confirm:

  • We only request TikTok API scopes necessary for our stated functionality
  • We do not use TikTok data to build user profiles for advertising
  • We do not share TikTok user data with third-party advertisers
  • TikTok OAuth tokens are stored securely and used only for publishing
  • We delete your TikTok tokens when you disconnect your account

You can revoke the App's access to your TikTok account at any time by going to TikTok Settings → Security → Manage Authorizations.

Section 05

Data Storage & Security

We implement industry-standard security measures to protect your data:

  • OAuth tokens are encrypted at rest and in transit
  • We use HTTPS for all data transmissions
  • Access to user data is restricted to essential personnel only
  • We conduct regular security reviews of our systems

Video content is only temporarily stored during the upload process and is not retained after successful publishing to TikTok.

Section 06

Data Retention

We retain your data as follows:

  • OAuth Tokens: Until you disconnect your TikTok account or revoke access
  • Publishing Logs: 90 days from creation
  • Video Content: Deleted immediately after successful upload to TikTok
  • Account Information: Until you delete your account

Section 07

Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access the personal data we hold about you
  • Request correction of inaccurate data
  • Request deletion of your data
  • Revoke TikTok access at any time
  • Withdraw consent for data processing

To exercise these rights, contact us at ifa403@gmail.com

Section 09

The App is not intended for users under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If we become aware that we have collected such information, we will delete it immediately.
