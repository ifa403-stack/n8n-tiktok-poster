02. Description of Service

Vicifa is a tool that lets creators connect their own TikTok account and prepare videos to upload to that account. Specifically, the Service allows you to:

  • Sign in with your own TikTok account using TikTok's official Login Kit (OAuth 2.0).
  • Upload a video file through Vicifa, which is sent to your TikTok account as a private draft using the TikTok Content Posting API.
  • Open the TikTok app yourself to review, edit, and decide whether to publish that draft.

Vicifa does not generate content, does not schedule posts, does not publish or share anything on your behalf, and does not connect to any TikTok Shop, analytics, or third-party automation service. Every video that becomes public on TikTok is published by you, manually, inside the TikTok app.

03. TikTok API Usage & Data Scopes

Vicifa requests the following TikTok API permissions, and only these:

  • user.info.basic — to display your TikTok username and profile picture inside Vicifa so you know which account is connected.
  • video.upload — to upload a video file to your TikTok account as a private draft only. This scope does not allow Vicifa to publish, share, or make any content public. Only you, using the TikTok app, can publish a draft.

Vicifa never requests the video.publish scope and never posts to your account automatically or without your direct, in-app action.

04. Account Connection & Your Control

You authorize the TikTok connection yourself through TikTok's official login screen, and you may revoke that authorization at any time from your TikTok account settings (Settings → Security → Manage account access) or by disconnecting your account inside Vicifa. Revoking access immediately stops Vicifa from uploading further drafts.
