Vicifa

A platform for TikTok creators to connect their own TikTok account and upload videos — securely via TikTok Login Kit.

Every user authenticates with their own TikTok account using OAuth 2.0. Your account, your content, your control.

Powered by TikTok API

How It Works

Any TikTok creator can sign up and use the platform independently. No sharing of accounts or credentials.

1

Connect Your Account

Log in with your own TikTok account using the official TikTok Login Kit (OAuth 2.0).

2

Upload Your Video

Select your video file and add details. Your content stays private until you publish it.

3

Publish as Draft

The video is saved as a draft on your TikTok account. You review and publish it yourself on TikTok.

Platform Features

🔒

Your Account, Your Control

Each user logs in with their own TikTok account via OAuth 2.0. The platform operator never posts on your behalf or has access to your content.

🎬

Easy Video Upload

Upload video files directly to your own TikTok account as drafts. Review and publish them whenever you are ready.

👤

Open to All Creators

Any TikTok creator can register and use this platform independently. Not limited to any specific team or individual.
